# EE209 Team [34] Charter

**Members:** [Jai, Uzayr, Noah, Emre] · **Date:** [31/07/2026]

## What we're aiming for

Top 5 team, and the Smart Energy Challenge board.

The only way that works is all four of us understanding the whole design. We get interviewed separately at every lab, at the progress review and at the end, so if one of us can't explain a part of it, that hits the team and not just that person. The roles below are about who builds each bit, not who understands it.

## Who's doing what

| Who | Owns |
|---|---|
| [ Jai ] | Analogue front end, power supply, PCB |
| [ Emre ] | Sampling and core firmware |
| [ Noah ] | UART, display, ESP32, app |
| [ Uzayr ] | Integration, testing, enclosure |

Owning a block means you build it and you're the first person someone asks about it. It doesn't mean you're the only one who touches it.

## What we're each signing up to

**Deadlines are firm, designs are up for debate.** We'll be strict about dates and freeze points. Anyone can argue about how something should work, and whoever owns that block makes the final call.

**Say something the same day you get stuck.** Not next standup, not next week. Quietly falling behind is the only thing that actually breaks a project like this, and it's fixable if we hear about it early.

**Turn up to standup.** Fifteen minutes, [day and time]. Done, next, blocked. If you can't make it, post your three lines in Slack.

**Push before every assessment.** Lab repos and the team repo. Assessors mark off GitHub, not off our laptops.

**Commit under your own account.** Nobody pushes work on someone else's behalf. The commit log is how our individual contributions get seen.

**Shout before you open a shared design file.** Altium, LTspice, Proteus and CAD files can't be merged. Post in the channel first, pull, then post again when you've pushed.

**Write decisions down.** If we decide something in a conversation, it goes in DECISIONS.md the same day. Two months from now none of us will remember why.

**Keep your own logbook.** It's individual and it gets marked.

**Do your own lab answers in you're own repo** 

**Help each other get across the whole thing.** Before the final interviews we each run a short walkthrough of our own area for the rest of the team.

## If something isn't working

1. Talk to the person directly, that week.
2. If that doesn't sort it, bring it to standup.
3. If it still isn't sorted, take it to a TA or the course director before the next peer assessment is due.

Nobody should ever find out there was a problem by reading about it in a peer assessment.
